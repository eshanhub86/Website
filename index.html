About

Educator. Researcher. Academic Leader.

I am Dr. Eshan Samanta, Ph.D. (Tech.), an academician, researcher and academic administrator with more than 16 years of professional experience in engineering education, research and industry.

I currently serve as Assistant Professor and Acting Dean (Admin), Electrical & Electronics Engineering at Bharat Institute of Engineering & Technology, Hyderabad, an autonomous institution with NAAC 'A' Grade.

My academic and research interests span intelligent fault protection, fuzzy inference systems, power systems, power electronics, IoT, renewable energy, smart grids, electric vehicles, automation, embedded systems and emerging AI-enabled electrical engineering applications.

My teaching experience includes Digital Signal Processing, Power System, Power Electronics, AutoCAD, AC Machines, Control Systems, Microprocessor & Microcontroller and interdisciplinary technology applications.

I am particularly interested in creating a positive, student-centred learning environment where teaching, research, innovation, professional societies and industry engagement work together to develop future-ready engineering professionals.
